## Design Context

### Users
NLP and ML researchers and practitioners attending, presenting at, or following the EMNLP 2026 tutorial "Failure Modes in Agentic AI". Three jobs, in order of weight:
- **Credibility and sharing**: presenters, organizers, and colleagues link to the site; it has to read as a serious, well-run tutorial at first glance.
- **Following along live**: during the 3.5-hour session, often on a phone or laptop in the room, people look up the schedule, which part is on, who presents it, and (later) the slides.
- **Reference afterwards**: people return for the reading list, slides, and materials.

### Brand Personality
Rigorous and editorial. Three words: **exacting, scholarly, unhurried**. It should feel like a well-typeset survey paper or conference proceedings volume: calm, precise, confident through typography and structure rather than decoration. Emotional goal: trust and clarity, not excitement.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: light. Read in daylight, in conference rooms, and alongside papers; the reference object is printed.
- **References**: a lab technical report or proceedings volume; firm sans headings over a book face for reading; hairline rules and whitespace instead of boxes; rubricated (red) section marks used sparingly.
- **Anti-references**: generic AI landing pages (centered everything, card grids, pastel or purple gradient blobs, pill buttons everywhere, typewriter animations), dashboard UI, "techy" monospace shorthand.
- **Type**: Archivo, semi-condensed (`font-stretch: 87.5%`), for the hero, section titles (700), and part, session, and reading-list titles and names (650); Archivo regular width for navigation, labels, metadata, and times (tabular numerals). Literata (optical sizes) for reading text, subtitles, and emphasis in italic. Chosen over a Caslon book pairing for scannability during the live session. No monospace.
- **Color**: OKLCH; neutrals tinted toward the brand indigo (hue about 277); indigo ink as the brand color; one vermilion rubric accent for section marks, stars, and failure labels; muted ochre and green only as semantic labels for diagnostics and fixes.
- **Constraints**: Astro static site on GitHub Pages; keep the existing section structure and content (polish in place); WCAG 2.2 AA contrast; respect prefers-reduced-motion; usable at phone width (16px gutters, no horizontal scroll); README style rule: no en or em dashes in source.

### Design Principles
1. **Typography carries the hierarchy.** Few sizes with clear contrast (at least 1.25 ratio), condensed sans for titles and wayfinding, serif for reading; emphasis by italic and weight, never by color alone.
2. **Rules and whitespace, not boxes.** Separate content with hairline rules, alignment, and spacing; reserve containers for things that are genuinely interactive.
3. **One consistent page grid.** A left meta column (part number, time, presenter) beside a content column, reused across Schedule, Outline, and Reading List so the page reads as one document.
4. **Fast to scan, live or later.** Schedule, part, and presenter are findable in seconds on a phone; nothing important is hidden at small widths.
5. **Accent is rare.** The vermilion rubric marks only what deserves attention; everything else is ink on paper.
